Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Vistonaut.

How does Vistonaut evaluate my profile strength?

Vistonaut analyzes your academic GPA, test scores (IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT), intended major, and document readiness across 4 key metrics: Academics, Exams, SOP, and Overall Profile Strength to help you evaluate your target fit.

How do Dream, Target, and Safe university categories work?

You can organize universities based on profile fit. Safe universities match or exceed your credentials, Target universities are a strong competitive fit, and Dream universities represent ambitious reach institutions.

What happens when I lock a university choice?

Locking a university commits it to your active application queue. This automatically unlocks personalized Application Guidance, generating a customized task list, required document templates, and milestone timelines.

Can I draft and save my SOPs directly in Vistonaut?

Yes! Vistonaut includes a dedicated Document Workspace where Statements of Purpose, Letters of Recommendation workflows, and Resumes automatically save to the cloud for each locked university.

Is the AI Counsellor available 24/7?

Yes. The AI Counsellor is always available with text and voice output to answer questions about country selection, exam preparation, document drafting, and application next steps.
